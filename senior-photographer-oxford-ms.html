Senior photographer in Oxford, MS, your questions answered

I'm Samuel Long, a senior photographer and videographer serving Oxford, MS and Madison, MS. I take senior pictures for high school seniors in Oxford and across Lafayette County, and for Ole Miss seniors on campus. Here's how a session works, where we can shoot and what each session includes.

By Samuel Long · Updated October 26, 2026

Three senior sessions

Essential

Thirty minutes at one location. You get at least 15 edited, high resolution photos in a private online gallery. It's one look at one spot.

Signature

A full hour at up to two locations with one outfit change. You get at least 30 edited photos, sized for printing and for posting. Cap and gown provided.

Premium

Two hours across several locations with two outfit changes and 40 or more edited photos. Pick this one if friends are coming along. Cap and gown provided.

Prices for all three are on my senior session pricing page. Not sure which one fits? Send me your date for a quote.

How a session works

Send me your date

Tell me which session you're thinking about and roughly when. I reply personally, usually within a day.

Plan the spots

We plan the spots together before the day. Essential stays at one location, Signature covers up to two and Premium moves across several. If you're changing outfits, Signature includes one change and Premium includes two.

The session

I'll tell you where to stand, so you don't need to know how to pose. If friends are coming, book Premium: it runs two hours across more than one location, so there's room for the group.

Your gallery

Your edited photos come to you in a private online gallery: at least 15 with Essential, at least 30 with Signature and 40 or more with Premium.

Where to take senior pictures in Oxford

The senior portraits on this page are from sessions on the Ole Miss campus: walking past the University of Mississippi 1848 gateway, on wide stone steps beside tall white columns, behind a bed of yellow daffodils in spring and on the field at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium.

Campus isn't the only option. I take senior pictures across Lafayette County too, so if there's a place that means something to you, bring it up when we plan the spots. My guide to places to take pictures in Oxford, MS covers the Square, Rowan Oak and Lamar Park, with the rules and hours I could find for each.

Graduating from Ole Miss? Ask me about a graduation session and we'll plan the stops together, at spots like the Grove, the Walk of Champions and the university seal. My guide to Ole Miss senior and graduation pictures has the cap and gown details.

Closer to Jackson, MS? I also take senior pictures around the Jackson, MS metro, including Madison, Ridgeland, Flowood and Brandon, and elsewhere in Mississippi on request. It's all on my page for senior pictures across Mississippi.

Photos and a hype film, one booking

I shoot both photo and video, so if you want senior pictures and a senior hype film, you only have to book one person. In the Jackson, MS metro, I've made hype films for Jackson Prep and Jackson Academy football and a preseason film for the Jackson Academy Encore show choir. Watch the hype films.

When you reach out, I reply personally, usually within a day. I'm an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot, and I've been making photos and films since 2019. More about me.

Questions seniors and parents ask

How much are senior pictures in Oxford?

I offer three senior sessions in Oxford: Essential, thirty minutes at one location; Signature, a full hour at up to two locations; and Premium, two hours across several locations. Current prices are on my senior session pricing page, or send me your date for a quote.

Where in Oxford do you take senior pictures?

On the Ole Miss campus and across Lafayette County. The Oxford senior portraits I show were taken on campus, at spots like the University of Mississippi 1848 gateway and wide stone steps beside tall white columns. We plan your spots together before the session.

How many outfits can I wear?

Essential is one look at one spot. Signature includes one outfit change, so you can wear two looks, and Premium includes two changes, so three looks.

Can friends join my senior session in Oxford?

Yes. Book Premium if friends are joining you: it's two hours across several locations with two outfit changes, so there's time for pictures on your own and with your friends.

Do I need to know how to pose?

No. I'll tell you where to stand, so you don't need to know how to pose.

Yes. For Ole Miss graduates, I take cap and gown pictures around campus at spots like the Grove, the Walk of Champions and the university seal. My guide to Ole Miss senior and graduation pictures covers the stops, the timing and how a session differs from the ceremony photos. On Signature and Premium, I bring the cap and gown.

Can you make a senior hype film too?

Yes. I shoot both photo and video, so senior pictures and a hype film can be one booking. In the Jackson, MS metro, I've made hype films for Jackson Prep and Jackson Academy football and a preseason film for the Jackson Academy Encore show choir.
