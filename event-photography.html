How I cover an event

Before the event we go over the timeline, the people who need to be in the photos and any group shots you want. Then I work the room: candids, the details you planned, the speeches and the dance floor.

For bigger events my SLP Media team brings two or four photographers. If you want video, I can film a short recap of the night, and I'm an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot, so I can add aerials where the venue and the airspace allow it.

Your edited photos come in the SelfSearch AI gallery, one private link that's easy to share with your guests. Guests find their photos with a selfie. The selfie search works like a face search, with no app needed and no account to make. SLP Media never saves the selfie, and the gallery stays up for a year with free downloads and an optional PIN.

Event photography questions

How much does an event photographer cost in Mississippi?

Event photos are booked as SLP Media packages, from my event photography team: one photographer for an hour at a party of up to 100 guests, two photographers for three hours at one venue for up to 500 guests, or four photographers for four hours with a lighting package. Every package includes the SelfSearch AI gallery and a custom photo border, and the prices are listed at slpmedia.pro. Video is quoted on its own. Send me the date, the venue and a rough guest count and I'll reply within a day.

Do you bring more than one photographer?

For bigger events, yes. SLP Media packages come with one, two or four photographers, and I'll tell you which fits when you send your guest count. A second room can add a photographer.

Can you film the event too?

Yes. I can film a short recap video alongside the photos and add drone footage where it's permitted. I'm an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot.

How do we get the photos?

Your edited photos come in the SelfSearch AI gallery, one private link you share with your guests; each guest takes one selfie and sees the photos they're in. I plan delivery within 2 days of the event (3 days for Monday to Thursday events), and your quote gives the date.

Where do you photograph events?

Anywhere in Oxford and around Ole Miss, from the Square and the Grove to private homes and venues out in Lafayette County. I also serve Madison, the Jackson metro and the rest of Mississippi.
