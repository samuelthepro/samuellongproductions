## Summary (Granola AI notes)

### Classical Bits vs. Qubits

- Classical digital systems: states are 0 or 1 (single dimension)
- Quantum systems use qubits: infinite possible states
  - Represented using ket notation: |V⟩ = a|0⟩ + b|1⟩
  - Coefficients a and b are complex numbers in general
- Qubit lives in 2D quantum space; any state expressible as linear combination of basis vectors

### Ket Notation and Quantum Basis

- Ket |V⟩ expressed geometrically as a point on a circle: |V⟩ = cos θ |horizontal⟩ + sin θ |vertical⟩
- Basis vectors |0⟩ = \[1,0\] and |1⟩ = \[0,1\] are orthonormal
  - Inner product of a vector with itself = 1
  - Inner product of two orthonormal basis vectors = 0
- Basis need not align with x/y axes: any two orthonormal vectors U and U⊥ can serve as a basis

### Inner Products and Bra-Ket Notation

- Inner product written as ⟨U|V⟩ (bra-ket): bra is the conjugate transpose of the ket
- Conjugate transpose: complex components are conjugated, column becomes row
  - Example: conjugate transpose of \[1,0\] = \[1,0\]; of \[i,0\] = \[-i,0\]
- Orthonormality confirmed by examples:
  - ⟨horizontal|horizontal⟩ = \[1,0\]·\[1,0\]ᵀ = 1
  - ⟨horizontal|vertical⟩ = \[1,0\]·\[0,1\]ᵀ = 0

### Measurement Probabilities

- To find coefficient a: take inner product of both sides with U
  - ⟨U|V⟩ = a (since ⟨U|U⟩ = 1 and ⟨U|U⊥⟩ = 0)
- Probability of measuring state |V⟩ as U: P(U) = a\* · a = |a|²
- Probability of measuring as U⊥: P(U⊥) = b\* · b = |b|²

### Exercise 2.2 and 2.4 Worked Examples

- Exercise 2.4: determine which state pairs represent the same state
- Part J (same state):
  - |V1⟩ = (1/√2)|0⟩ + e^(i5π/4)|1⟩ and |V2⟩ = (1/√2)e^(-iπ/4)|0⟩ + …
  - V2 = e^(-iπ/4) · V1: scalar multiple, so both represent the same state
- Part C (different states):
  - |V1⟩ = (1/√2)(|0⟩ + |1⟩) and |V2⟩ = (1/√2)(-|0⟩ + i|1⟩)
  - Cannot obtain one from the other by scalar multiplication: different states
  - Measurement probabilities for both: P(|0⟩) = 1/2, P(|1⟩) = 1/2 (same despite being different states)
  - Note: same probabilities for different states is not always the case
