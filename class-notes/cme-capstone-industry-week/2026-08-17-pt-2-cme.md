## Summary (Granola AI notes)

### FMEA and Risk Priority Overview

-   FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) introduced as a risk prioritization framework
-   Risk Priority Number (RPN) = Severity × Occurrence × Detection (each scored 1–10)
    -   Severity: 1 (no effect) to 10 (endangers operator without warning)
    -   Occurrence: 1 (failure unlikely) to 10 (failure almost inevitable)
    -   Detection: 1 (automatic detection) to 10 (no known controls to detect failure)
-   RPN ranks which failure modes to address first
-   Example: seat belt wrong color scored severity 5, probability 4, RPN 144
-   PFMEA (Process FMEA) focuses specifically on how a process can fail
