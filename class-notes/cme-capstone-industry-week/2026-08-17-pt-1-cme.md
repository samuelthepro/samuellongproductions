### Problem-Solving Tools

-   Fishbone diagram (Kaoru Ishikawa)
    -   Categories: Man, Machine, Material, Methods, Management, Mother Nature
    -   First-cut brainstorm: get all team ideas on contributing factors
    -   Circle 2-3 most likely causes to carry forward
    -   Must appear in Thursday presentation
-   5 Whys
    -   Ask “why” successively until no further why exists: that’s root cause
    -   Apply to the 2-3 circled fishbone items
    -   Must appear in Thursday presentation
-   8D Process (Kepner-Tregoe, developed for Ford)
    -   Used by ~80% of manufacturing companies (Viking Range, Huber, Holmac, etc.)
    -   Incorporates fishbone and 5Y as early steps
    -   Eight disciplines:
        1.  Form the team (done: 7 people, diverse backgrounds)
        2.  Define the problem statement (frame tightly: only what you’re solving)
        3.  Containment action (band-aid to stop the bleeding; never a permanent fix)
        4.  Root cause identification via Is/Is Not table
        5.  Develop permanent corrective action
        6.  Implement corrective action
        7.  Prevent reoccurrence
        8.  Recognize the team
    -   Is/Is Not table: every is/is-not pair must be true for a possible cause to be root cause; one false pair eliminates that candidate
-   FMEA (brief touch): problem-avoidance technique used by military and aerospace
-   A3 (Toyota): mentioned briefly; not widely used outside Toyota, not a focus
