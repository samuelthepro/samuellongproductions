## Summary (Granola AI notes)

### The Sermon’s Central Claim

-   Every follower of Jesus has been given spiritual authority, not earned but received at salvation
-   Two extremes to avoid: cluelessness (unaware of authority) and recklessness (overstepping it)
-   Authority flows through alignment: salvation, identity, and life all centered in Christ

### The Organizational Chart of Authority

-   Common wrong views:
    -   God and Satan as equals fighting over us (false)
    -   God above Satan, with us beneath both (true only without Christ)
-   Correct view: life is hidden with Christ in God (Colossians 3:3, John 14:20)
    -   “In Christ” appears 160 times in the New Testament
-   Authority is not ours; it is Christ’s, delegated to us as we live under him

### Four Truths About Spiritual Authority

-   Authority is received at salvation, not earned through spiritual maturity
    -   Illustrated by Jed Clampett: he became a millionaire when he bought the land, not when he discovered the oil
-   Authority is exercised only when life is aligned with Jesus
    -   Salvation, identity, and life must all be in alignment for authority to flow
    -   Sons of Sceva (Acts 19) tried to cast out demons without authority and were overpowered
-   Authority is attached to mission
    -   Disciples were sent out; authority accompanied the going (Matthew 10:5-7)
    -   Authority does not flow through self-centered living; it flows through ambassadors on mission (2 Corinthians 5:20)
-   Authority enables supernatural outcomes on earth
    -   Spiritual warfare: resist the devil and he will flee (James 4:7); renounce permissions given to darkness
    -   Healing: Jesus gave authority to heal every kind of sickness; testimonies shared from Josh and Jason
    -   Raising the dead: rare but documented in Acts and modern missionary accounts (Dr. Jerry Rankin, IMB)
    -   Speaking blessing: authority to speak peace and life over people wherever you go (Matthew 10:12)

### Why Healing Doesn’t Always Happen

-   The person’s heart may not be ready (John 5: “Do you want to be healed?”)
-   God’s greater plan may not yet be visible (John 9: man blind 40 years, healed at the right time)
-   A thorn in the flesh may serve humility and dependence (Paul, 2 Corinthians 12)
-   Sometimes it is simply a mystery; Paul left friends sick despite his own healing ministry
-   Ultimate kingdom victory is resurrection, not just healing in this life

### Closing Call to Response

-   Invitation to salvation: move from the kingdom of darkness to the kingdom of light
-   Invitation to healing: pray in faith, seek elder prayer if led
-   Invitation to deliverance: repent, renounce permissions given to darkness, receive fresh filling of the Spirit
    -   “In Jesus name, I renounce the permission I’ve given you. You have no authority in my life.”
-   Closing song: “I Speak Jesus,” declared over areas where freedom is needed
