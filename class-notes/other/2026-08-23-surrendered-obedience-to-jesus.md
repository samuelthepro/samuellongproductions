## Summary (Granola AI notes)

### The Example of Jesus: Surrendered Obedience

-   Central claim: if meeting Jesus didn’t change your life, you met religion, not Jesus
-   Jesus modeled surrendered obedience by being filled with and led by the Holy Spirit
    -   Laid aside divine “cheat code” (Philippians 2: emptied himself)
    -   Lived from the inside out: Spirit directed his thinking, choosing, and desire
-   Garden of Gethsemane as the defining example
    -   Jesus didn’t want to suffer, yet prayed: “Not my will, but yours be done”
    -   Key takeaway: Jesus didn’t always feel like obeying. He just chose to

### The Call to Discipleship (Luke 9:23)

-   Three requirements to follow Jesus:
    1.  Deny yourself: stop living a self-directed life, refuse fleshly desires
    2.  Take up your cross daily: embrace God’s specific assignment for your life
    3.  Follow Jesus: move toward what Christ has for you
-   Only two kinds of people: those who surrender to God, and those who live for self
-   Denying yourself is a command, not a suggestion, and must be chosen personally

### Three Circles: Assessment of Where You Are

-   Based on 1 Corinthians 2, three ways to live:
    -   **Circle 1 (Natural Man):** Christ outside your life; self-directed, spiritually clueless, separated from God
    -   **Circle 2 (Fleshly/Infant Christian):** Christ in your life but not in control; stuck, frustrated, spiritually immature
        -   Most miserable place: can’t enjoy sin because of Jesus, can’t enjoy God because of sin
        -   Paul’s warning: examine yourself to confirm you’re truly in the faith
    -   **Circle 3 (Spiritual Man):** Christ at the center; surrendered, discerning, unaffected by others’ opinions
-   Theologically, only two states exist (in or out), but the normal Christian life is Circle 3

### How to Live in Circle 3: Love and Perspective

-   Love is the primary motivator for obedience
    -   John 14:21: keeping Jesus’s commands is the evidence of loving him
    -   Cycle: love God → obey → God reveals more → love grows → obey more
    -   The more you grasp how much you’ve been forgiven, the more you love (Luke 7 woman at Jesus’s feet)
-   Shift in perspective required
    -   Stop building your own kingdom; enter and serve in Christ’s kingdom
    -   Keep the end in mind: Jesus is returning in glory, not as a servant but as a king
-   Gratitude as a daily anchor: “God, thank you for grace this week”

### Stories of Surrendered Obedience

-   Daughter Rachel: felt God say sorority rush “wasn’t for her”; chose obedience over desire at 17
    -   Parents’ principle: better to follow what you think is God’s voice than to learn to ignore it
-   Lisa TerKeurst on a plane: gave away her personal, annotated Bible to a stranger named Ron
    -   Ron read it cover to cover, gave his life to Jesus, led his family to faith
    -   24 years later, his daughter found Lisa and showed her Ron’s notes alongside Lisa’s own
-   Demon-possessed man (Mark 5): sent home to tell 10 cities what Jesus had done
    -   Impact came not from following Jesus physically, but from simple obedience to go home and testify
