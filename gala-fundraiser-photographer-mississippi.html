Planning the gala

How many photographers does a gala need?

That depends on the guest list. Up to 100 guests, one photographer covers it. Over 100, two photographers can cover up to 500 guests at one venue, and a bigger gala gets the full team: four photographers and the lighting package. A cocktail hour or an auction in a second room can add one more photographer.

What do you need from us before the gala?

A planning call about the rooms, the run of the evening, the guest count and the photos you need most. Send the honorees, sponsors and speakers who have to be in the photos, and tell me when the speeches, the honoree presentations and the auction happen, so we're in position.

How much does gala photography cost?

Gala photography is booked as an SLP Media package priced by guest count, and every package includes the SelfSearch AI gallery and a custom photo border. The prices, extra hours, extra photographers and the lighting package are on the SLP Media packages page.

What holds our date?

Half up front: a 50% deposit, invoiced when you book, holds the gala date, and the balance is due 7 days before the event.

Do you photograph galas in Jackson, MS?

Yes. I work from Madison, MS as well as Oxford, so SLP Media covers charity and nonprofit galas and fundraisers in Jackson, Madison, Ridgeland, Flowood and Brandon, and in Oxford, on the Ole Miss campus and across Lafayette County. A venue more than about 30 miles from either city gets a travel quote.

On the night

What do you photograph at a gala?

The arrivals, the room, the details you planned, the speeches, the auction and the people you'll want to thank afterward. We stop for the group shots on your list, like the honorees and the sponsors.

Can you set up a photo station?

Yes, with the lighting package. Professional lights go up at your backdrop or a spot we pick together, so the posed photos stay consistent from the first arrivals to the last. It's part of the full team package and an add-on for the two smaller ones, and one photographer stays on the station while the others cover the room.

Can you film a recap of the gala?

Yes. I shoot recap films myself, as Samuel Long Productions, and a gala film can be booked with the photos and quoted on its own. As an FAA Part 107 pilot, I can add drone aerials where the airspace and the venue allow it, never over the crowd.

What if the program runs long?

We stay with it. Every extra hour is billed for each photographer, so if the program is likely to run late, add the time to the quote up front.

After the gala

When will the photos be ready?

Within 2 days of the gala, or 3 days if it's on a Monday to Thursday, the edited gallery is ready to send to guests and sponsors. Your quote gives the exact date, and later you get a summary with the numbers from your gallery.

How do guests and sponsors get their photos?

You share one private link with your guests and sponsors. Each person opens it, takes a selfie and sees the photos they're in, with no app or account needed. Anyone who signs up with the QR code at the door gets the link by email.

Can the gala's name go on the photos?

Yes. The custom photo border comes with every package: your organization's logo, or up to three lines of text like the gala's name and the date, on the photos guests save, beside the SLP Media mark.

What happens to the selfies and the gallery?

Each selfie is used once to match a guest to their photos and is never saved, and anyone can opt out of matching or ask for a photo to come down. The gallery stays up for a year with free downloads and an optional PIN. More on privacy is in my event photographer answers.
