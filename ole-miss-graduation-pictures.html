Where on the Ole Miss campus do you take graduation pictures?

I take Ole Miss graduation pictures at spots like the Grove, the Walk of Champions and the university seal. The Grove is also where graduates in academic regalia sit for Convocation, according to the Ole Miss graduates guide. If another corner of campus meant something to you, add it to the list and we'll plan the stops together.

Which session should I book for graduation pictures?

For graduation pictures, it comes down to how many stops you want. Essential is thirty minutes at one location, with up to 15 edited, high resolution photos in a private online gallery. Signature is a full hour at up to two locations with one outfit change and up to 30 edited photos, sized for printing and posting, so you could wear the cap and gown at one stop and change for the next. Premium is two hours across several locations with two outfit changes and 40 or more edited photos. With Signature and Premium, I bring the cap and gown.

Do I need to know how to pose for graduation pictures?

No. I'll tell you where to stand, so you don't need to know how to pose. Just bring any cords or stoles you'll wear at graduation.

Do you provide a cap and gown?

Yes, on Signature and Premium. I bring a cap and gown, so you don't have to wait for yours to arrive. Bring any cords or stoles you want in the photos. On Essential, bring your own cap and gown.

Can family and friends be in my graduation pictures?

Yes. Premium is the one to book if friends or family are joining, since it runs two hours across several locations. That leaves time for pictures on your own and pictures with the people you bring.

How much do Ole Miss graduation pictures cost?

Ole Miss seniors book the same Essential, Signature and Premium sessions on campus. Current prices are on my senior session pricing page, or send me your date for a quote.

Ceremony photos or a portrait session?

For commencement photos, the Ole Miss graduates guide points graduates to gradimages.com. GradImages photographs the ceremony. A portrait session is the other half: the campus pictures you plan yourself.

Do I still need a session if GradImages photographs the ceremony?

GradImages ceremony photos and a portrait session are different pictures. GradImages covers the ceremony, and that's where you'll find your commencement photos. A portrait session is planned pictures around campus at the spots you choose, with time at each stop, outfit changes on Signature and Premium, and room for friends and family on Premium. If you want pictures at the Grove or the seal, that's what a session is for.

When to take Ole Miss graduation pictures

When should I take my graduation pictures?

Before commencement or after it. Pick what suits you and send me the date. The Class of 2027 is made up of graduates whose degrees are conferred in December 2026, May 2027 and August 2027, and the Ole Miss graduates guide says 2027 graduates keep their caps and gowns instead of returning them. With Signature and Premium I bring a cap and gown, so you don't have to wait for yours from Grad Supply. On Essential, pick a day after yours arrives.

Can we take pictures back home instead of in Oxford?

Yes, if home is around the Jackson, MS metro. Besides Oxford, the Ole Miss campus and Lafayette County, I take senior pictures in Madison, Ridgeland, Flowood and Brandon. If you're somewhere else in Mississippi, send me the details.
