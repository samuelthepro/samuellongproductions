Date party and formal photos

How many photographers does a date party need?

At least two. Date parties are dark and packed, so my SLP Media team brings two photographers and splits the room. Two photographers cover three hours at one venue for up to 500 guests, and past 500 guests the full team brings four.

What do you shoot?

The posed pictures everyone asks for and the candids: couples and friend groups, the details the social chair spent all week on and the dance floor.

Can we get lit photos at a backdrop?

Yes. The lighting package can be added for posed photos at your backdrop: professional lights, so a photo looks the same at the start of the night as at the end.

Can our letters go on the photos?

Yes. The photos guests save carry a custom border with your letters or logo, or up to three lines of text, like the chapter name and the date.

How much do date party photos cost?

Date party and formal photos are booked as an SLP Media package by guest count, and every package includes the SelfSearch AI gallery and a custom photo border. The prices are on my Greek life photo pricing, and a 50% deposit holds the date.

Date party and formal films

Do you film date parties and formals?

Yes, as Samuel Long Productions. Date parties and formals share one video package: four hours filmed from several angles, cut into a 30 to 60 second highlight reel plus the aerial footage, color graded with custom music. Prices are on my Greek life video pricing.

Does the film include the drone?

Yes. It's the one Greek package where I bring the drone. I'm an FAA Part 107 pilot, and when the weather and the location allow it, you get aerial footage with your reel.

Can you fly at a chapter house?

Every flight on Ole Miss property goes through UPD for approval, and many chapter houses sit on university property. I'm authorized to fly on campus and file the request myself, so tell me at least a week ahead. On a home football game day no drone can fly within 3 nautical miles of Vaught-Hemingway Stadium from an hour before the game until an hour after. Here's how the Ole Miss drone rules work.

Should we book photos, the film or both?

Book photos if you want a full library for members to share, and the film if you want a short reel for Instagram and TikTok. You can book both.

After the party

How do members and dates get their photos?

On one private link the chapter shares. Each guest opens it, takes one selfie and sees the photos they're in, with no app and no account. A QR sign-up at the door emails the link to each person who signs up.

How fast?

For photos, we plan delivery within 2 days of the event, or 3 days for Monday to Thursday events, and your quote gives the date. For the film, ask me about timing when you reach out.

Do you keep the selfies?

No. Guests agree before the camera opens, each selfie is used once, and SLP Media never saves it. Anyone can ask to be left out of matching or for a photo to come down.

Can we keep the gallery private?

Yes. Anyone with the link can browse and download for free for a year, and the chapter can add a PIN if it wants the gallery private.
