Questions

What if our event runs longer?

Extra time is billed by the hour, and the hourly rates are on my pricing page. If you know the event will run long, tell me up front and I'll build it into your quote.

Can you bring more than one photographer?

Yes. Date party photos always come with two photographers, and for parents weekend, bid day and other large chapter events I bring two or three and quote on guest count, hours and locations.

Should our chapter book photo or video?

Book photos if you want a full library for members to share, and video if you want a short reel for Instagram and TikTok. You can book both, and for parents weekend I can combine photo and video in one quote.

How fast do we get our photos and video?

House event photos and house event reels come back within one week, and pregame photos come back on a quick turnaround. For date parties and formals, ask me about timing when you reach out.

How do we book?

Send me the date and the event through the contact form or email samuel@samuellongproductions.com. I reply personally, usually within a day.

Got an event on the calendar?

Tell me the date and what the chapter has planned, and I'll tell you what fits.
